Research experience

Aug 2021 – present

Research scholar

IIT Madras · Advisor: Prof. R. I. Sujith

Experimental and dynamical characterisation of critical transitions to periodic oscillations in turbulent thermo-fluid systems. A key contribution is the experimental discovery of a strange nonchaotic attractor in a complex turbulent reactive flow system.

Sep – Nov 2025

Visiting researcher

Potsdam Institute for Climate Impact Research (PIK), Germany

Applied recurrence quantification analysis to the transition from chaos to order in a turbulent thermoacoustic system, under Prof. Norbert Marwan.

Jun 2020 – May 2021

Master's research

Investigated synchronization and phase locking in a candle-flame oscillator subjected to external acoustic forcing.

Research

Research interests

My doctoral work, supervised by Prof. R. I. Sujith, examines critical transitions in turbulent thermo-fluid systems with an emphasis on the emergence of periodic oscillations.

01

Thermoacoustic instability

Investigating the dynamical routes to oscillatory instability in turbulent combustors and how the nature of a transition changes with operating conditions.

02

Nonlinear dynamics & complexity

Characterising strange nonchaotic attractors, transitions from chaos to order, recurrence properties and collective dynamics in complex systems.

03

Experimental fluid mechanics

Combining high-speed imaging, laser-based diagnostics and time-series and spatiotemporal analysis to investigate turbulent flows.
